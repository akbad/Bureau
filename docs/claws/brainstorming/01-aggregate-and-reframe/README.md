- In particular, we need to choose between one of the options below.

## Options

> These options are not exhaustive and are completely open to modification or addition of new options

For both of concierge and auto skill evolution, we need to choose one of these options:

### 1: deletion

Delete these homemade implementations and allow them to be subsumed by whichever of the claws platform(s) we add on to Bureau.

### 2: hybrid

Completely/partially keep these implementations for any number of reasons (e.g. such as uniqueness of our own featureset or another compelling reason to keep developing some or all of that functionality) *alongside* incorporating 1 or more of the claws platforms 

### 3: continue

Completely keep these implementatinos (without adding any claws platforms) and develop them as our own answer to the claws platforms (i.e. due to our desire to implement a refined/combined/merged/superset version of 1 or more of the claws platforms from scratch via concierge, the dynamic skill tooling, and other future work)
