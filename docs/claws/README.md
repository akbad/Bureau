# To claw or not to claw

## What this dir is for

I want, in particular, to aggregate the [independent exploratory research tracks](exploration/README.md) to use it for our own assessment/brainstorming for the scenario where:

- we **drop** claude-mem, and
- we **decide** whether to drop the [concierge functionality](concierge/) (and maybe the autonomous skill evolution that's [beginning to be scaffolded](/protocols/context/dynamic/skills/)) as a function of whether we should just let their functionality/responsibilities be either:

    - subsumed by whichever of the claws platform(s) we add on to Bureau
    - completely/partially kept for any number of reasons (e.g. such as uniqueness of our own featureset or another compelling reason to keep developing some or all of that functionality) *alongside*
incorporating 1 or more of the claws platforms
    - completely kept (without adding any claws platforms) and developed as our own answer to the claws platforms (i.e. due to our desire to implement a refined/combined/merged/superset version of 1 or more
of the claws platforms from scratch via concierge, the dynamic skill tooling, and other future work)
