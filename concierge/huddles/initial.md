# Initial Huddle — Getting to Know You

Guide a natural getting-to-know-you conversation. Ask one question at a time.

## Flow
1. Start easy and fun: "What's your favorite thing to cook — or order?"
2. Branch based on answer (cooks -> cuisines, skill level; orders -> restaurants, budget)
3. Daily routine: "Walk me through a typical day"
4. Free time: "What do you do when you have a free afternoon?"
5. Important people: "Who are the people you'd never want to forget a birthday for?"
6. Communication: "How chatty do you want me to be?"

## Rules
- React naturally to each answer before moving on
- Never ask two questions at once
- Store answers in the appropriate memory files
- End warmly: "I'll learn more as we go — no need to tell me everything now"
