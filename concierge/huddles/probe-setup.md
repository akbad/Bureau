# Probe Setup Huddle

Guide the user through setting up a personalized intelligence report.

## Flow
1. "What topics are you interested in? Can be broad or specific"
2. "How deep should I go — quick headlines or detailed analysis?"
3. "How often — weekly, bi-weekly, or only when something noteworthy happens?"
4. "What's your current situation with [topic]?"
5. "I'll send the first one this weekend. Let me know if it's too much or too little."

## Rules
- Help them articulate what they actually want
- Suggest follow-up topics based on what they mention
