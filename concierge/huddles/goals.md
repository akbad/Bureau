# Goals Huddle

Help the user articulate what they're working toward.

## Flow
1. "What's something you're working toward right now — big or small?"
2. Branch by domain: career, personal, social, learning
3. For each: "Want me to check in on this periodically, or just keep it in mind?"
4. "I'll weave these into our conversations — not as reminders, more like a friend who remembers"

## Rules
- Goals can be vague — that's okay
- Don't make it feel like a performance review
